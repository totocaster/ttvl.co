About These Walks

Some days left a route, photographs, and a few words. Others left only fragments. Gaps in a recording stay visible; a photograph without a reliable position stays in the sequence.

Digital photographs are placed using their geotags or, where possible, their capture time. Film photographs follow the roll sequence; their scan dates are not treated as the moment they were taken.

The habit behind these days appears in Walking and the 2022 retrospective: long walks, café breaks, and time to think.
