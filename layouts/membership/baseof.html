Why Join / What You Get

By becoming a ROAM member, you're saying you want to see more of my work in the world—and I'm grateful for that. However, I also understand that people want something in return. To address this and to motivate myself to be more productive, I created Folio Editions, which are mini photo books featuring the projects I'm working on. I'll mail physical copies to yearly members and send digital versions to monthly members once they're ready. My goal is to produce a few each year.

Folio Editions

All yearly members receive a physical mini-book, which I call Folio Editions, featuring projects I'm currently working on. These may be complete long-form projects or works in progress each edition marking a milestone. Sometimes, they're smaller projects I'm curious about in the moment, and a mini-book format is the perfect way to share them.

Folio Editions are printed here, in Japan, and shipped from my Tokyo studio worldwide—wherever airmail delivery of printed matter is available—at no extra cost to yearly members. Yearly members also receive a 50% discount on any Folio Editions produced before they joined.

Monthly members receive a digital version of Folio Editions week after they are dispatched to yearly members.

Frequently Asked Questions

I've never heard of you. Where should I start?

Check out ttvl.co for essays and resources on creativity and craft. If photography is more your thing, head to toto.photo.

If you're curious about my technical workflow, visit the darkroom section of my website; I share quite a bit about general photography there.

I also post a lot of information about the bookbinding skills I'm learning here in Tokyo from the master bookbinder, Nao Inoue.

If you're into productivity, take a look at my hybrid notebook system, Unbound, which combines analog and digital tools, creating what I believe is the most open and flexible thinking tool out there.

For a broader view of my projects, The Flâneur Newsletter is free and an excellent way to get a sample of my work.

Does ROAM stand for something? Why all-caps?

Yes, it's an acronym—or rather, a "backronym." It stands for Research of Artbook Membership, which reflects the experimental nature of this membership program. I also just like the word "Roam."

Roam Press is an indie book design and fine art bookbinding venture I started alongside the membership program. All Folio Editions and books I create—whether for myself or for others—are marked with the Roam Press wordmark starting 2025.

Who handles payments?

Memberships are managed through Memberful and Stripe (including Apple Pay)—both secure and widely trusted services. I never see your payment details. You can cancel anytime with just a couple of clicks.

I'm a student and short on funds. Any options?

There's no dedicated "student tier," but if you email me at toto@roampress.co with a few words about yourself, I'll grant you a free yearly membership (minus the physical Folio Editions). I don't want cost to prevent anyone genuinely interested in my work from participating.

Will your content go behind a paywall?

No. ttvl.co will remain my public-facing notebook, where I share my curiosities and ongoing projects.

I'm signing up for a yearly membership and being asked for my mailing address. Why?

Yearly members receive a physical—real, tangible, made of paper and ink—copy of the Folio Edition.

If you don't want a printed copy of the Folio Edition, consider either switching to a monthly subscription that doesn't require any additional information, or send me a message and we'll sort it out.

I will never sell your address or phone number to any data broker.

I'm planning to get a yearly membership and live in [X country]. Will I receive the Folio Edition?

Folio Editions are printed and mailed from my studio in Japan, using Japan Post's "printed matter" Airmail service, which delivers to most countries worldwide. However, there are some restrictions due to various global conflicts. Please consult the Japan Post website to confirm whether your country can receive mail from Japan.

What's the Jinny Street Gallery mentioned in the yearly membership perks?

Jinny Street Gallery is a small outdoor street gallery I operate in the Jingumae 2-chome district in Harajuku, focusing on emerging artists. All yearly members get a 10% discount at the Gallery Store—whether you're buying merchandise or artwork by our artists.
