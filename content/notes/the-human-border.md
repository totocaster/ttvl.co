---
title: "The Human Border"
date: 2026-05-07
category: thinking
---

My rule for AI use is a border of interaction.

Before the border, in private, I am happy to use AI as much as it helps: search, planning, code, transcription, translation, summaries, and any other tool-shaped assistance.

The border is other people. Once something leaves my side --- an email, a message, a published note, a photograph, or maybe someday a piece of music --- it has to be authored by me, with my hands and brain.

I don't ever want anyone to question whether any message from me is authored by me or by AI. 

This note is that contract.
