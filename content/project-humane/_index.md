Project Humane is an umbrella term for my made-for-hands tools.

Currently part of project humane efforts are:

- [Plaintext Commons](/plaintext-commons/): Working manifesto for keeping notes, tools, and agents aligned around open files.
- [Arrowhead CLI](/project-humane/arrowhead): A Rust-powered search and automation layer for plain-text vaults that keeps notes accessible to both humans and AI agents.
- [Stamp CLI](/project-humane/stamp): Filename helper that keeps Plaintext Commons vaults consistent for humans, Arrowhead, and automation.
- [Unbound Notebook System](/notebook-system/): Flexible, software- and bran-agnostic note-taking system designed for free thinking and exploration based on loose leaves.
- [Soma](/project-humane/soma): A paper note transcription and digitization tool, now partially superseded by Arrowhead's planned analog-ingest workflows.
