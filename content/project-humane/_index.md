title: Project Humane

Project Humane is an umbrella term for my tools for thought and made-for-hands efforts.

## Ideological Projects

- [Plaintext Commons](/plaintext-commons/): A working manifesto for keeping notes, tools, and agents aligned around open files.

## Analog Tools

- [Unbound Notebook System](/notebook-system/): A flexible, software- and brand-agnostic note-taking system designed for free thinking and exploration, based on loose leaves.
- [Keychain EDC](/project-humane/edc-keychain)

## Digital Tools

- [Arrowhead CLI](/arrowhead/): A search and discovery layer for plain-text vaults, making notes accessible to both humans and AI agents.
- [Stamp CLI](/stamp/): A filename helper that keeps Plaintext Commons vaults consistent for humans, agents, and automation.
- [Soma](/project-humane/soma) _(currently being reworked)_: A paper note transcription and digitization tool, now partially superseded by Arrowhead's planned analog-ingest workflows.
