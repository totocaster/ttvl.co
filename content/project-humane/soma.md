_Soma is an iOS and macOS app that scans, transcribes, and digitizes analog notes._

**Soma is not yet released and remains a work in progress.**

I originally designed Soma to work with my [Unbound System](/project-humane/notebook-system/), but it quickly became evident that the app is system-agnostic. It works seamlessly with any note-taking system and, in fact, with any handwritten pen-and-paper notes.
