**Before Kyojima — February 28, 2023.** In 2023, the apartment was both my home and workspace. The studio and darkroom had not yet moved to Kyojima, and the things needed for daily life and creative work still occupied the same rooms. The scan records that overlap before work was given a separate place elsewhere.

**After Kyojima — August 3, 2026.** The second scan captures the apartment after the working parts of my life moved to Kyojima. Daikyocho had become solely a living space: still the same home, but no longer also responsible for being a studio and darkroom.

Together, the scans do not document a renovation so much as a shift in function: the same apartment before and after work was separated from home.

## Location

Daikyocho is a small district in Shinjuku, close to Shinjuku Gyoen. This apartment has been my Tokyo home since February 2021.
