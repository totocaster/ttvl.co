On September 19, 2026, Ludo and I celebrated our wedding with a private party at Mutant Radio in Tbilisi. For the occasion, we brought back _Wirescapes: Connected With the Urban Fabric_, our joint exhibition first shown at UNTITLED SPACE in Tokyo in November 2022.

_Wirescapes_ brings together my urban photography and Ludo’s work with reused technological materials. We made the original show before becoming a couple; revisiting it for our wedding celebration brought that shared beginning into a new space. This scan records the cube just before disassembly, a few days after the celebration.

[More about the Wirescapes reprise →](https://toto.photo/exhibitions/wirescapes-reprise/)

## Technical Note

I made this scan with an iPhone 17 Pro. As impressive as the results are, I found the phone limited for scanning a space this large. I built this splat from frames taken from three minutes of video, shot in less-than-ideal lighting.

I’m quite enjoying the process, though, and might consider getting a dedicated scanning camera to document exhibitions and places.
