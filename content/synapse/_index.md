title: Synapse
---

A note-taking software ecosystem that aims to bridge the gap between analog and digital thinking tools, creating a unified workflow for capturing, processing, and connecting ideas. While designed to integrate with the [Unbound Notebook System](/notebook-system/), Synapse can be used as a purely standalone note-taking app—UNS support is just one of many aspects being explored.

**Synapse is in the early development and research stage** and was spawned from [Soma.app](/project-humane/soma/) as the project evolved beyond its original scope. The project encompasses multiple applications and tools that work together to support various aspects of knowledge work and creative thinking.
