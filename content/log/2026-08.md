- Released [Refmode CLI](/project-humane/refmode/), a macOS command-line tool for switching Apple display reference modes from Terminal, scripts, and hardware controllers, with version 1.0 available through [GitHub](https://github.com/totocaster/refmode) and Homebrew.
- Published [_Ship the tool, not the agent_](/notes/ship-the-tool-not-the-agent/), an argument for exposing useful capabilities as clear CLIs and APIs that people and agents can both use.
- Shipped version 7.8 of [ttvl.co](/), a large redesign made almost entirely of small details: standardized typography and color roles, a filtered [Projects archive](/projects/), a year-by-month [Log archive](/log/), terminal-style command blocks, framed tables, unified resource lists, and a rebuilt [Links page](/links/).
- Created an unpublished, interactive before-and-after 3D record of my Tokyo home in 2023 and 2026 for a future [Traces](/traces/) entry about how the space changed when work moved elsewhere.
- Spent much of the month rebuilding _HyperContext_, a work-in-progress system for giving people, applications, and AI assistants a durable shared context layer.
- Sent the remaining shipment from the [Kyojima studio](/traces/kyojima-studio/) to Italy, finishing another practical stage of the move.
- Spent two weeks working remotely and traveling across Bali, moving through Balangan, Candi Dasa, Canggu, and Ubud.
- Visited palaces, temples, and gardens across Bali—including [Tirta Gangga](https://disparda.baliprov.go.id/tirta-gangga/2020/04/) in the east and Goa Gajah near Ubud—and made a photo study of Balinese doors and gates along the way.
- Joined a bamboo architecture and carpentry workshop at [The Kul Kul Farm](https://www.kulkulfarmbali.com/) and made two bamboo trays for the studio.
- Turned a 20-hour layover into a brief visit to [Chengdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu) before returning to Rome.
- Continued setting up the Rome studio, including building a large worktable with a top made from 3 cm birch plywood.
- Completed the main application paperwork for my Italian residence permit.

August happened almost entirely in transit: from Tokyo through Bali and Chengdu to Rome, with another suitcase already packed for Tbilisi. Somehow, it was also a productive month. The last Kyojima shipment left Japan, Refmode became a real tool, HyperContext moved forward, and ttvl.co received the sort of redesign where everything changes and almost nothing looks different.

The bamboo workshop was a useful counterweight to all the code, planes, and forms. Making two simple trays slowly by hand felt unusually luxurious. It was also a convincing reminder that not every useful tool needs a command-line interface; occasionally it can just be a piece of bamboo.

Rome is beginning to feel like home rather than a place I am setting up between trips. The studio has a proper worktable, my tools are arriving, and even the residency paperwork has advanced from abstraction to bureaucracy—which is how Italy confirms that something is becoming real.
