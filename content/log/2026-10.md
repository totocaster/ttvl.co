- Launched the new [Jinny Street Gallery website](/jinny-website/). Not going to lie, I'm quite proud of it. 
- Redesigned ttvl.co again. This time it actually looks different. I switched to IBM Plex throughout and made the dates and labels consistent.
- Built an interactive [3D twin of the Rome studio](/research/ambient-computing/studio-twin/) for **Studio Humane** and started documenting my [ambient computing experiments](/research/ambient-computing/) in a new [research journal](/research/).
- Started building [Hank for OpenClaw](/research/hank/), a native OpenClaw client for Mac, iPhone, and iPad, and gave it its own stream in the journal.
- Added [Walks](/traces/walks/) to Traces, bringing GPS routes, selected photographs, and journal excerpts together in an interactive archive.
- Sent 17th The Flâneur dispatch: [_A Room to Work In_](/flaneur/017/).
- Published [_Software gets malleable when describing it is enough_](/notes/software-gets-malleable/), [_Square sensors make sense for online video_](/notes/square-sensors/), [_There won’t be an app for that_](/notes/there-wont-be-an-app-for-that/), and [_I’d rather play with the plan than read it_](/notes/id-rather-see-the-plan-than-read-it/).
- Wrote to more than 80 photobook publishers about [_Scent of Time_](/flaneur/014/) ahead of the [FotoFilmic exhibition in Paris](https://toto.photo/exhibitions/fotofilmic-book-program-paris/). Grateful to the few who replied.
- Started building **Plate**, a macOS and iOS app that turns content from my website and Jinny into Instagram posts, reels, and stories using templates.
- Began planning **Maquette**, a tool for organizing photographs into books and exhibitions.
- Took down Onree’s [_Memory Foam_](https://www.jinnystreetgallery.com/exhibitions/memory-foam/) at Jinny Street Gallery.
- Spent two days helping a friend and his father bottle, cork, and label wine in Georgia. I was the labeler, so if those bottles reach a shelf next year, I labeled every one of them.
- Went to the [4GB Music Festival](https://4gb.ge/) in Tbilisi, then returned to Tokyo for the rest of the month.

October was the month every software idea I’ve carried around for years showed up at once. I let most of them in. The AI agents have been a great help with the programming, so naturally I’ve given myself several more things to design. I wrote at the start of the month that [software gets malleable when describing it is enough](/notes/software-gets-malleable/). I didn’t mention that the person doing the describing wears out. By the third week I was falling asleep at 4 a.m. and running through AI subscriptions in days.

Meanwhile, Tokyo has started coming apart in small, official ways. I’ve been dealing with the tax office and city hall and finding a new home for my 3D printer. Next stop is Paris, with the maquette, 200 postcards (I may have overdone it), and a long list of publishers I hope to meet.
