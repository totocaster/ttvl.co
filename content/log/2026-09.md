- Celebrated our wedding at Mutant Radio in Tbilisi and brought back [_Wirescapes_](https://toto.photo/exhibitions/wirescapes-reprise/) for the occasion. Built a new welded steel frame for the installation and [documented the result](/traces/wirescapes-mutant-radio/).
- Released [Copy Editor](/project-humane/copy-editor/), a local writing tool that keeps editing suggestions beside my draft while leaving the writing to me.
- Continued developing _Hypercontext_ and its native clients for macOS and iOS.
- Contributed a [Georgian translation](https://github.com/andymai/gridfinity-layout-tool/pull/4205) to Gridfinity Layout Tool, merged on September 10.
- Completed the first draft of the new [Jinny Street Gallery website](https://beta.jinnystreetgallery.com/). Really excited about this one.
- Built an interactive [specification page](https://beta.jinnystreetgallery.com/specs/) for the gallery's streetlamp display case, so artists can explore its dimensions and plan their installations.
- Published [_The Other Side of the Gate_](/flaneur/016/), a Flâneur dispatch about the doors and gates of Bali.
- Researched publishers ahead of presenting [_Scent of Time_](/flaneur/014/) in Paris.
- Spent three days in Yerevan and returned to Tbilisi via Lake Sevan.
- Went to [Stockton Invites 2026](https://www.instagram.com/stocktoninvites/) at Satibe, where [Moku-Moku](https://mokumoku.bandcamp.com/) were a highlight.
- Attended [Giorgi Nebieridze](https://nebieridze.de/)’s talk at the [Tbilisi Photography and Multimedia Museum](https://tpmm.ge/en/).
- Watched [_The Odyssey_](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt33764258/) and really enjoyed seeing it on a big IMAX screen. Not sure I'm going to rewatch it, though.

After the wedding celebration in Tbilisi, I feel like I’m a little past the halfway mark on the insane list of things I decided to do this year. I’m not into astrology in the slightest, but Ludo says it’s the Year of the Fire Horse, and, oh boy, it definitely feels like being strapped to one.

A weight is off my shoulders now that the party at Mutant Radio, one of our favorite places in the city, is behind us. I can slowly ease back into the rhythm of normal life. I miss photo projects, making physical things, and being in a studio. On the software side, though, I’ve been productive, which does keep the creative worm in my brain happy.

I still have a massive project ahead of me: moving out of Tokyo in January and wrapping things up there. For some reason, it doesn’t feel as hard as everything else I’ve done this year. And then there is Paris Photo, which I’m actually excited about. Onward, forward.
