- Developed new sections for [toto.photo][tp]—exhibitions, press coverage, and a works archive.
- Had three photographs from my Tskaltubo series published in the [Atlas of Ruins][ar] book.
- Migrated [The Flaneur][tf] newsletter platform to ttvl.co; no more Substack and walled gardens.
- Updated ttvl.co: the [darkroom][dkrm] section now has several new items, and the [Unbound System](/project-humane/notebook-system/) section has been expanded with new templates.
- Learned a new technique for negative-to-positive conversion in Photoshop using negative mask subtraction—still fine-tuning the results.
- Built and started experimenting with a pinhole camera.
- Started working on a membership website platform using Go + Gin.
- Took a long walk in the Pigneto district in Rome.
- Visited the Vatican Museums; Raphael's School of Athens and the Sistine Chapel are impressive.

2025 started off very busy. I've made good progress towards my plan to launch a membership program alongside three(!) websites. I feel like I'm on track to achieve that. The 12-week year system is working well for me, although I do not adhere to its original methodology from the book. I didn't find [the book][twy] quite good, frankly—but the idea is well worth trying.

The visit to Tbilisi was pleasant as usual, but the moment I was there, [the protests][tbsp] were much calmer than I expected. I didn't manage to shoot much.
