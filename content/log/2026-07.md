- Launched [*Traces*](/traces/), a new series of scans, memorabilia, documents, and other records of my life and work, beginning with [*Kyojima Studio, 2024–2026*](/traces/kyojima-studio/)—an interactive 3D scan preserving the studio just before packing began.
- Added [*A Spatial Long Exposure*](/traces/spatial-long-exposure/) to the series, collapsing 38,403 points from my location history and geotagged photographs into a single undated map.
- Finished a second, updated hand-bound maquette of [*Scent of Time*](/flaneur/014/) for its Paris remount during Paris Photo week.
- Published [Patchcord](/project-humane/patchcord/), an agent-friendly workflow for building and debugging CircuitPython projects.
- Finished packing the Kyojima studio’s books, tools, and equipment for the move to Italy.
- Secured an Italian Type D long-stay visa.
- Spent three days at [rural 2026](https://ruraljp.com/en/); [Jane Fitz](https://ruraljp.com/en/projects/jane-fitz/)’s five-hour all-vinyl set was the musical highlight.
- Revisited [*Hiroshi Sugimoto: Extinction*](https://www.momat.go.jp/en/exhibitions/hiroshi-sugimoto-extinction) at the National Museum of Modern Art, Tokyo.
- Escaped central Tokyo twice for riverside breaks near Musashi-Itsukaichi and in Urasa, Niigata.
- Started rewatching the extended editions of [*The Lord of the Rings*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)).
- Revisited [*Dune: Part Two*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt15239678/), which held up better on a second viewing.
- Watched [*Exit Through the Gift Shop*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1587707/) for the first time. It was amazing; I am surprised I had somehow missed it until now.

July was a month of preserving things at the exact moment I was taking them apart. I made a spatial record of the Kyojima studio mere minutes before I packed 17 surface-mail parcels weighing more than 400 kilograms. Apparently a creative practice also has a freight category.

With the visa finally in hand, the move to Italy entered its least abstract phase: shelves, tools, books, and departures. I’m a bit terrified that I have to do it all again at home in January. I do have slightly fewer books there, so fingers crossed it’ll be easier.

The festival, rivers, exhibitions, and films kept the month from turning into a logistics marathon.
