--- title: 'The Flâneur Newsletter' ---

A newsletter about my work, photography, books, making, and walking.

The Flâneur is a little more private, a bit more experimental that what I publish on the website — and, if I may lean into the obvious metaphor, a little more wander-y. Not everything shared there will appear here, and you'll find more work-in-progress updates too.

Once a month. One click to unsubscribe.

Is this the same as the Substack version?

It was. But in the spirit of greater control and privacy, I've decided to move the newsletter to a self-hosted solution powered by Campaign Monitor. This way, your emails won't end up in the hands of billion-dollar companies trading them around, and I — admittedly a bit of a control freak — can have complete ownership over the design and experience. The Substack version is now defunct. And don't worry, if you ever wish to unsubscribe, it's still just one click away.
