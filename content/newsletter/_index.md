## What to expect from the newsletter?

Every month, I’ll share an update on what I’ve been working on, reading, and creating. Most of what ends up on this website—essays, guides, objects, and more—starts in the newsletter, often weeks before it’s published here in its final form.

I also see the newsletter as the best way to stay connected with you. As the world drifts away from RSS and social media becomes increasingly noisy, email feels like a more reliable and personal way to keep in touch.

## Is this the same as the Substack version?

It was. But in the spirit of greater control and privacy, I've decided to move the newsletter to a self-hosted solution powered by Campaign Monitor. This way, your emails won't end up in the hands
of billion-dollar companies trading them around, and I — admittedly a bit of a control freak — can have complete ownership over the design and experience. The Substack version is now defunct. And
don't worry, if you ever wish to unsubscribe, it's still just one click away.
