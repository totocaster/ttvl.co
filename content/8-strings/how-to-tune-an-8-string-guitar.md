title: How to Tune an 8-String Guitar
date: 2026-05-26
description: "A practical tuning note for making low 8-string guitar strings sit in tune"
---

Standard 8-string tuning adds B and F# below regular 6-string tuning:

```text
F# B E A D G B e
```

Drop E lowers only the 8th string:

```text
E B E A D G B e
```

Drop E feels familiar because of the low E, but the bottom of the fretboard maps differently because rest of the fretboard is fifth apart, not a fourth.

## Tune the low strings slightly flat

The important part: centered tuner readings can still make the lowest strings sound sharp against octaves and fifths. The string is technically at pitch, but the instrument does not sound settled.

- String 6: `E`, about -2 cents
- String 7: `B`, about -5 to -6 cents
- String 8: `F#`, about -10 to -11 cents

For Drop E, use the 8th-string offset as the starting point for E1. Heavier strings may need to sit lower. Treat the numbers as a first pass, then listen.
