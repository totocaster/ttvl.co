I approached the Contax G2 with caution. Its electronic nature felt daunting, especially for a camera of its age, but I was drawn to the modern features after years of shooting with purely mechanical Leica M2. Experimenting with the Fuji Klasse had already shown me how capable electronic cameras could be, yet I still wanted something more versatile.

After having some of those late 90s camera I relalized that photography YouTube community is, mostly, an echochamber of opionions rather than something coming from long-ter experiance. (William Sheepskin's [4-year experiance][sheepskin] video is the only G2 video you need to watch.)

Contax G System offers excellent wide-angle lenses—particularly the Biogon 21mm and 28mm—while remaining straightforward to operate. After trying these lenses, I was sold on the system. It hits the perfect balance between reliability and modern convenience, proving that all- electronic cameras still excel.

I own two G2 bodies, which I call _Daido_ and _Araki_, just to keep them distinct in my film log. If you’re interested in the system, I recommend the champagne versions. The black ones are unbelievably expensive and don’t offer any advantage beyond bragging rights. In Japan, they can cost twice as much as the champagne models, and matching black accessories is even harder—sometimes fetching triple the usual price. Good luck finding a GD-2 databack for a black model (I also have two of those).
