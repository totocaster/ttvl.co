I'm trying to understand what good software engineering looks like now that LLMs and coding agents are part of the work. The industry feels like it's shifting dramatically, and some of the tools and practices I've relied on are starting to feel awkward. Git, pull requests, and code review are among them. I still use them, but I increasingly question whether the workflows around them fit how the work now happens.

I don't yet know which practices need adapting and which need replacing. How should we organize changes, review work, and decide whether software is ready to ship? Where does human attention matter most? What does a good development process look like when an agent can take on much of the implementation?

This stream records my attempts to work that out through building software: the workflows I try, the tools that help or get in the way, and the assumptions that hold up—or don't—in daily use.
