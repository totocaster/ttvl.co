A short list to start [Home Assistant](/research/ambient-computing/home-assistant/) with:

- Pause a room's air conditioner when its door has been open for two minutes.
- A Leaving scene that turns off the lamps and both ACs and reports anything still open.
- A warning when the corner with the resin printer drops below about 20 °C.
- Pre-cooling on hot mornings, since the frontage faces east.
