We have a lot of drawers and shelves, and I move between two or three cities and more than one studio. I regularly forget what I have and where it is.

What I want is to ask the studio "where is the soldering iron?" and get an exact answer: the place in the virtual studio, so I can walk over and pick it up in the real one. Just as often the question is whether I own something at all, before I buy it twice.
