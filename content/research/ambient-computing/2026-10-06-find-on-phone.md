The phone gets a small web app from the studio brain, added to the home screen, with no App Store. Find searches the catalog. In the mockup's example catalog, "solder" turns up three things in cart drawer C2·3, and Show on wall screen flies the wall screen into that drawer.

The same answer could reach the room itself: when a search lands on the IVAR wall, the two shelf spots above it could brighten. The phone's other tab, Capture, is where [cataloging by photo](/research/ambient-computing/catalog-by-photo/) happens. Both are steps toward [finding the soldering iron](/research/ambient-computing/where-is-it/) for real.
