Before anything [can be found](/research/ambient-computing/where-is-it/), every place needs a name. The grammar:

- Rooms get one letter: L for the Lab, S for the Study, D for the Darkroom, P for the Passage, and W for the WC. The two lofts are M1 and M2.
- Fixed storage units take the next letter in their room: LA is the IVAR wall in the Lab.
- Slots work like a chessboard. Letters go across from the left as you face the unit, and numbers go up from the floor. LA·c3 is bay c, shelf 3.
- Things that move carry their own number. The four ALEX carts are C1–C4, and a cart's drawers count up from the floor: C2·3.
- Gridfinity bins inside a drawer can add grid coordinates when it helps: C2·3/f4.
- Search accepts lac3, la c3, or LA·c3.
