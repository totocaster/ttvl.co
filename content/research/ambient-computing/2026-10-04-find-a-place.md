The viewer is rebuilt around search. Type an [address](/research/ambient-computing/addresses/) in any of its spellings and the camera flies to it, the slot lights up in the model, and a card shows the unit with that slot filled in. Number keys pick a view, and the slash key jumps to search.

Every card still says "Not catalogued yet," because the places exist and the things don't. [Filling them in](/research/ambient-computing/catalog-by-photo/) is the next layer.
