Both desks in the Study, Ludo's by the window and mine at the back, adjust from 70 to 120 cm, so their height is part of the studio's state. The viewer gives each one a fader, and the sliding door gets one too. Moving a fader moves the desk in the model.

If the desks or the door ever report their position, the same faders become read-outs.
