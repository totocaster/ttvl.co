Computation that lives in the room instead of behind glass. The question behind this stream is how much of everyday computing can leave the screen and still feel ordinary: displays, voice, macropads, paper, and hands, spread around a room, instead of one device that wants all the attention.

This stream continues _Project Manus_, which began in 2023 with a custom macropad and moved to a ceiling-mounted projector and camera in 2025. In Rome, the work starts from the room itself: [_Studio Humane_](/research/ambient-computing/studio-twin/), a digital twin of our studio with Home Assistant underneath and screens, panels, keys, and voice around it.

The thinking behind it is in four notes: [Hands Are Not Cursors](/notes/hands-are-not-cursors/), [Headsets and Glasses Are a Stepping Stone at Best](/notes/headsets-and-glasses-are-a-stepping-stone-at-best/), [Attention Platforms Cannot Invent New Interfaces](/notes/attention-platforms-cannot-invent-new-interfaces/), and [Software gets malleable when describing it is enough](/notes/software-gets-malleable/).
