The first surface is an always-on display showing the model in cutaway, slowly turning. Every lamp is a spotlight aimed at what it lights, in the white or RGB color that Home Assistant reports. When a door sensor opens, the door swings open in the model. Around it sit the time and daylight, the rooms, the doors, the machines, recent activity, and a banner for anything that needs attention.

It's read-only and shows no camera feeds. When the studio is empty or the sun is down, it dims to ink. Which display, where it hangs, and what drives it are still open.
