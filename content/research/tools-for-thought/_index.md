How people think with tools, and how tools can stay out of the way. This stream covers plain-text systems, notebooks, and software that keeps files in the open, where people, applications, and agents can all read them.

Finished tools live in [Project Humane](/project-humane/). This is the research behind them: experiments, small findings, and questions that haven't settled yet. Related: [Plaintext Commons](/project-humane/plaintext-commons/) and the [Unbound Notebook System](/project-humane/notebook-system/).
