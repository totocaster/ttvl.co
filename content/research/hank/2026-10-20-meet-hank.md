Hank is the character for the [OpenClaw client I'm building](/research/hank/why-im-building-hank/). He's a small coral octopus with eight short arms, glasses, and a bow tie.

{{< figure class="plain" src="/visuals/research/hank/2026-10-20-hank-turnaround.png" alt="Three full-color renders of Hank, a coral octopus with black glasses and a bow tie, seen from the front, above, and the side" caption="Hank from the front, above, and the side." loading="lazy" >}}

The face studies keep the glasses and bow tie fixed, and change the eyes and mouth. A small smile, closed eyes, or an open mouth gives him a different expression without changing the rest of the shape.
